An under-complicated email service

We left the bells and the whistles at home. I don't think you'll miss them.

There is a lot of emphasis put on “doing things professionally” which was fine when “professionally” wasn’t strictly attached to over-complicated software.

We used to craft marketing campaigns and hold our breath as it launched. Now we argue over how many creatives it takes for Meta to change a lightbulb.

We used to write letters with words that we strung together. Now we’re like marketing toddlers playing with our toy blocks and crying when Outlook walks over and knocks it down.

We used to try really hard to build the best websites. Now we find ourselves swimming in the spittle of the talking heads who can’t stop drooling over agentic whats-its.

Now I’d like to claw some of that back.

Together.

In an attempt to cut down on button pushing and learn what pushes your customers’ buttons, we stripped everything that is unnecessary to sending and delivering an email out of idiosync.

That means it might suck sometimes, but it won’t suck your time.

You might pin it down as just the infrastructure of an email app. And that is fair. It is that. There is no visual builder so you have to paste your HTML email into the editor. But we are all drinking the AI koolaid right now, so have a sip while Claude or ChatGPT whips up your email in HTML. And then you…

Oh, you can send a test email… and then you blast a blast or enable an automation.

And then you get the f*** out of there. There is nothing else to do. Take a lap. You’ve conquered the world.

Call us, we will have a virtual beer together.

I think that is all. If I forgot something. Consider it’s hanging out with the bells and the whistles…

— Shep
