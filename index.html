What it does

Three things.

Each one does the obvious thing in the obvious place. There is no second way to do any of them.

Blasts

Write it, pick who gets it, send it. Schedule it if you want. No template gallery to get lost in.

Automations

Welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase. The three that actually earn their keep, set up in a minute each.

Sign-up form

One form, one popup, your list grows. No targeting rules, no exit-intent, no spin-to-win.

What it doesn't do

On purpose.

Everything below is something other email apps charge you for and you have never once opened. If any of it ships, it comes off this list.

  • AI subject-line suggestions
  • Visual journey builders
  • Predictive send-time optimization
  • Lead scoring
  • Template marketplaces
  • Spin-to-win popups
  • SMS, push, and direct mail
  • A dashboard you have to interpret
